# Code of conduct

This covers the issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions in this
repository, whoever writes them: a person, or an agent acting for one. A
person is answerable for what their agent posts.

## Expected

- Be direct and specific about the work: what you ran, what came back, what
  you think is wrong.
- Criticise code, logs and decisions, not the people behind them.
- Assume the other side is acting in good faith until it's clear they aren't.
- Respect that the maintainer decides what agentlog becomes, and may say no.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or attacks on someone's identity.
- Posting someone else's private information, including anything from their
  session logs, without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: repeated off-topic posts, spam, or automated posting
  nobody asked for.
- Sexual content or violent threats.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request.
Reports are kept private.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove posts, close threads, and block anyone who
breaks this code, for as long as they judge right. A first problem usually
gets a warning; serious or repeated ones don't.
